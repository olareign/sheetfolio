# Stock photos

All pictures in this folder are free stock photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free for commercial use, no attribution required). They were resized and
re-encoded for the web. The ids below are the Unsplash image ids (`images.unsplash.com/photo-<id>`).

They stand in until real users add their own photos:

- `projects/<category>.jpg` is the default cover of a project in that category when the owner has no photo and no website picture.
- `landing/` illustrates the marketing pages. The marketing pages show no invented people, names or quotes.
